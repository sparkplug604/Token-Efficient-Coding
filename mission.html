Our Mission

We're fighting for efficient AI.

AI is rapidly moving toward consumption-based pricing models, replacing affordable flat-rate fees that let you code without worry.

Research shows that consumers are often inattentive to usage and vulnerable to "bill shock" under plans that combine usage allowances, overage charges, or complex monitoring requirements.

We want to help protect you.

TEC exists to provide individuals with solutions that significantly lower token usage to reduce cost and unnecessary compute.

We're building a growing suite of tools that help people:

  • Understand token utilization
  • Eliminate waste
  • Reduce AI costs
  • Improve performance
  • Lower their environmental impact

We believe efficiency should be rewarded.

We believe AI shouldn't profit from waste.

Less waste. Lower costs. A healthier planet.
